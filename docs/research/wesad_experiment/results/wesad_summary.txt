── TEXTO PARA O PAPER (Section 6 — Empirical Validation) ───

We validated LICET's Layer 3 (Mahalanobis distance) on the WESAD
benchmark dataset [Schmidt2018], comprising N=XX subjects undergoing
the Trier Social Stress Test (TSST). Using 60-second windows with 50%
overlap, we extracted RMSSD from wrist photoplethysmography (BVP, 64 Hz),
tonic/phasic EDA decomposition via cvxEDA [Greco2015], temperature
deviation, and 8-12Hz accelerometer power, building individual baselines
from the pre-stress resting phase.

The LICET protocol achieved sensitivity of 89.6%
(±26.9%) for stress detection and specificity of
48.2% for the amusement condition,
yielding balanced accuracy of 68.9%.
Mean RMSSD decreased from 288.9 ms (baseline) to
361.8 ms (stress) (Wilcoxon p=0.0256).
Mahalanobis D² increased from 2.83 to 176.04
(Cohen's d=0.50, Wilcoxon p=0.0001).

To test the GAP-B01 paced-breathing limitation, we evaluated the periodicity index on the guided-meditation condition — the genuine state physiologically closest to a paced-breathing attack. The index flagged 6.6% of meditation windows as suspicious.

── LIMITATIONS (wrist-PPG RMSSD reliability) — texto pronto para Seção 6.4 ──

Two results depart from the a priori hypotheses and warrant explicit discussion
rather than being reported as unqualified positive findings. First, mean RMSSD
increased rather than decreased under acute stress (H4), contrary to the
established direction in the HRV literature [Thayer2012]. An accelerometer-based
motion-artifact rejection gate (window excluded when wrist ACC energy exceeded
the subject's own resting median + 3 MAD) removed 0-35% of windows per subject
but did not correct the direction, ruling out gross movement as the primary
cause. This is consistent with Sinichi et al. [Sinichi2025], who validated the
same wrist device (Empatica E4) across a battery of cognitive and emotional
tasks and found RMSSD accuracy collapsing under mental arithmetic (r=0.31 vs.
ECG reference) — a task structurally similar to the TSST arithmetic component —
attributing the degradation to subtle speech- and muscle-tension-related
artifact rather than gross motion. A broader meta-analysis [Xu2026] confirms
RMSSD and HF power as the HRV metrics most prone to systematic PPG-derived
error even after refined processing. Second, specificity for the amusement
condition (48-50%) fell short of the 70-85% expected range. We initially
hypothesized general arousal ambiguity between amusement and stress; this is
not well supported — amusement is associated with lower heart rate and higher
RMSSD than fear, anger, or neutral states [Wu2019], i.e. it is physiologically
closer to relaxation than to stress on the HR/RMSSD axis. The better-supported
mechanism is narrower: electrodermal activity is valence-blind by physiological
design, rising under both positive and negative high-arousal states alike
[Kreibig2010], while RMSSD — the signal that should differentiate the two — is
itself unreliable in this wrist-PPG extraction for the reasons above. The
GAP-B01 periodicity result, which does not depend on absolute RMSSD magnitude,
is not subject to this confound and is reported as a positive finding without
qualification.
